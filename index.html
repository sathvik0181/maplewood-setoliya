Welcome to our Satoliya League!

What started a few years ago as a fun weekend gathering has grown into an incredible local community tradition! Satoliya is an ancient, action-packed street game from India that balances strategy, running, accuracy, and quick teamwork.

Whether you are a seasoned veteran looking to support our friendly matches or a beginner eager to learn, there is a place for you here. We play regular games right here in the community at Maplewood Park. Come down to meet the players, experience the culture, and jump into the action!

How To Play: The Official Rules

1. Setup & Teams

We pile up 7 flat stones from largest to smallest at the center of the field. Players are divided cleanly into two squads: Seekers and Hitters.

2. The Opening Throw

A Seeker stands behind the line and throws a tennis ball to knock down the stone pile. They get three chances. If they miss all three, the teams switch roles immediately.

3. Rebuilding the Tower

Once the stones fall, the Seekers must rush to stack all 7 back up in order. Meanwhile, the Hitters retrieve the ball and try to tag the Seekers out by hitting them below the shoulders.

4. Winning a Round

If the Seekers successfully rebuild the pile, they shout "Satoliya!" and score 1 point. If the Hitters tag all Seekers out before the pile is rebuilt, the Hitters win the round.

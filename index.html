Welcome to our Satoliya League!

What started as a fun weekend gathering has grown into an incredible local community tradition! Satoliya is an action-packed street game that balances strategy, running, accuracy, and quick teamwork.

We play regular games right here in the community at Maplewood Park. Come down to meet the players, experience the culture, and jump into the action!

7-Stone Rainbow Tower

How To Play: The Official Rules

1. Setup & Teams

We pile up 7 flat stones from largest to smallest at the center of the field. Players are divided cleanly into squads: Seekers and Hitters.

2. The Opening Throw

A Seeker stands behind the line and throws a tennis ball to knock down the stone pile. They get three chances.

3. Rebuilding the Tower

Once the stones fall, Seekers must rush to stack them back up in order. Hitters retrieve the ball and try to tag them out below the shoulders.

4. Winning a Round

If Seekers successfully rebuild the pile, they shout "Satoliya!" and score 1 point.
